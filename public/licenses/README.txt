sliso bundles Leaflet (BSD 2-Clause) and locally served Manrope / DM Sans fonts (SIL Open Font License). Full notices accompany these files. Geographic/traffic data have separate dataset-specific licenses and attributions. Dataset terms and provenance are described in data/README.md in the repository; the app manifest lists its active sources. The derived walking index is attributed to OpenStreetMap contributors under ODbL; GTFS Regional data is CC0 via Trafiklab/Samtrafiken.
